How they join

  • Access comes before everything on Track 3. The instance cannot be documented, the code cannot be audited and the upgrade cannot be rehearsed until we hold the right logins — which is why that step is the one already moving.
  • Track 3 does not block Track 1. The assistant sits outside Odoo, so the version 20 work neither holds it up nor is complicated by it.
  • Track 2 touches Track 3 once, at the very end — Odoo sends its mail through whichever provider wins.
  • Each track needs its own key. Three different logins, three different owners — none of them unlocks another.
